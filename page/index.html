Beta Quest Seed Explorer

A seed analyzer for Beta Quest Bingo

The BingoSync seed number, from 1 to 999999.

Quick Introduction

Analyze any seed that corresponds to a Beta Quest filename seed and a BingoSync seed (range 1-999999). This will generate the BingoSync board for that seed, then use a model of the Beta Quest seed to find routes to each goal and score the difficulty of each goal.
